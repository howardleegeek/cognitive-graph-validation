## Key Architectural Discoveries

### What Works
1. **Unified architecture** — 22% physical, 78% semantic (H1)
2. **32k+ dimensions** with α=0.3 regularization (H1.20)
3. **Graph structure** for temporal reasoning (H2.x: +56-75%)
4. **Invariant learning** for cross-dynamics transfer (H1.8: +5.4%)
5. **Concatenation** over attention for simple tasks (H3)

### What Doesn't Work
1. **Attention mechanisms** — Concat simpler and better (H3)
2. **Two-branch fusion** on complex tasks (-31.1%) — H1.10
3. **Multi-task dynamics** training — makes transfer worse (H1.9)
4. **Cross-dynamics transfer** with vanilla unified — fails by -56.7% (H1.4)

### Combined Approach (New Best Practice)
- Use **Graph + Invariant** architecture for tasks requiring:
  - Cross-domain transfer
  - Temporal reasoning (object permanence)
- Currently achieves: +10% transfer + 45% temporal
