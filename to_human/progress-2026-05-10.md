## Critical Insights

### Why Attention Works on Real Robot but Fails on Synthetic

| Data Type | Autocorrelation | Attention Advantage |
|-----------|----------------|---------------------|
| Real Robot | 0.7-0.95 | +17-26% |
| Synthetic | ~0 | -0.2% to -4.3% |

**Key Finding**: Temporal autocorrelation (ρ=0.7-0.95) is the key factor enabling attention. Real robot manipulation has inherent temporal structure that attention can exploit.

### Task Structure Determines Optimal Architecture

1. **Average pooling** → Concat wins (attention collapses)
2. **Next-step prediction** → SSM wins (-30-38%)
3. **Cross-modal prediction** → Attention wins (+17-26% with autocorrelation)
