# Round 293: H1.2.1 — Length-Conditioned Structural Prior Memory

H1.2.1 tested whether making the structural prior strength (alpha) length-conditioned could fix the seq_len=30 failure from H1.2. The sub-hypothesis predicted that alpha should scale inversely with sequence length — stronger prior for shorter sequences where temporal context is sparse. A sweep over beta (0.0–1.0) showed that even minimal conditioning (beta=0.20, boosting alpha from 0.65 to 0.74 at seq_len=30) brings the SPM/CG underfit ratio from 1.21x down to 1.14x, crossing the <1.15x threshold. At beta=1.00, seq_len=30 ratio drops to 0.94x (SPM actually beats CG). H1.2.1 is SUPPORTED: length-conditioning is a simple, effective fix requiring no architectural changes.
