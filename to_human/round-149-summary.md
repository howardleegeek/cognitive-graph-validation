# Round 149 Summary: Hierarchical Subgoal Decomposition

**Experiment H1.378** tested whether hierarchical planning with subgoal decomposition could help CG handle 4+ step tasks, after H1.377 showed external memory scaling failed on longer horizons. Results show **partial support**: CG with hierarchical subgoal decomposition achieved **+2.5% improvement on 4-step tasks** (MSE 0.357156 vs baseline 0.366249), while a plain hierarchical planner without CG structure actually hurt performance (-2.9%). This is the first positive result on 4-step tasks in this research thread. The modest improvement suggests the combination of CG + hierarchy is promising but needs refinement—future work should explore more aggressive decomposition (3 subgoals for 4 steps) and learned subgoal representations.
