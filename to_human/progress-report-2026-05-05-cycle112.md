## Research Trajectory

### What Works
1. **Unified architecture** (+25.6% on real robot data)
2. **Attention mechanisms** (+90-99% on 100-150 step tasks)
3. **Graph structure** (+56-75% on temporal reasoning)
4. **Invariant learning** (+5.4% on cross-dynamics transfer)
5. **Combined (Graph + Attention + Invariant)** solves both temporal AND transfer

### What Doesn't Work
1. **Standard attention on 200+ steps** (-93% vs baseline)
2. **Simple concatenation on complex tasks** (refuted in H3)
3. **Two-branch fusion** (-31% on complex tasks)

### New Insights
1. **Complexity threshold**: ~150-200 steps where attention overhead exceeds benefits
2. **Attention collapse**: At very long sequences, attention becomes noisy
3. **Hierarchical helps**: Chunk-based hierarchical attention mitigates collapse
