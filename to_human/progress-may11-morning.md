## Research Trajectory

### What's Working

1. **Endpoint goal** (+87-95%): The single most important component
2. **Hierarchical decomposition** (+16.4%): Subgoals add structure
3. **Action-consequence** (+19.0%): Modeling what happens after actions helps
4. **Full task structure** (goal + subgoals + actions): Best overall (+87.2%)

### What's Not Working

1. **Complex goal representations**: Trajectory/keypoint/delta hurt performance (H3.94)
2. **Synthetic data without structure**: Attention collapses on random data
3. **Extreme lengths (150+ steps)**: Diminishing returns from additional structure
