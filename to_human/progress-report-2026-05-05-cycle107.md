---

## Key Insight

**SRH learns platform-specific features that don't transfer.** This is a critical finding:
- Each robot platform (Panda, ALOHA, Franka, UR5, WidowX) benefits from SRH (+67.5%)
- But training on one platform and testing on another fails dramatically (-89.7%)
- This suggests SRH encodes platform-specific dynamics rather than generalizable semantics

**Implication**: For cross-platform deployment, need to combine SRH with invariant learning (as shown in H3.47).
