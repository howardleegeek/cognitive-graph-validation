# H1.470.1.1: Fine-grained dimension sweep around 768

## Hypothesis

There exists an optimal representation dimension (~768) for Cognitive Graph on multi-step tasks. This optimal point balances:
- **Below 768**: Representation is too constrained, limiting expressivity for multi-step reasoning
- **Above 768**: Overfits single-step tasks, showing diminishing returns on multi-step tasks

## Background

From H1.470.1 (dimension sweep [256, 512, 768, 1024]):
- **768** showed best multi-step improvement: +8.45% CG improvement, +4.00% gap
- **1024** overfits single-step: +18.11% single-step vs +8.52% multi-step, -9.59% gap
- **512** showed: +0.83% single-step, +2.67% multi-step, +1.84% gap
- **256** showed: +4.50% single-step, +0.28% multi-step, -4.22% gap

Key insight: Non-monotonic relationship with peak at 768.

## Prediction

Fine-grained sweep around 768 [640, 704, 768, 832, 896] will show:
1. **Peak multi-step improvement** around 768
2. **Single-step improvement** may continue increasing with dimension (overfitting)
3. **Improvement gap** (single-to-multi) minimized around optimal dimension
4. **Baseline s2m change** stable around 46-48% (as seen in H1.470.1)

## Experimental Design

- **Dimensions tested**: [640, 704, 768, 832, 896]
- **Tasks**: Single-step vs 3-step prediction
- **Samples**: 800 train, 200 test per dimension
- **Epochs**: 15 with early stopping (patience=5)
- **Runs**: 3 runs per dimension for statistical significance
- **Fixed ratio**: Maintain ~28% physical / ~72% semantic split across dimensions

## Success Criteria

**SUPPORTED** if:
- 768 shows highest multi-step improvement OR
- Improvement gap minimized at 768

**REFUTED** if:
- Another dimension (e.g., 832 or 896) shows better multi-step performance OR
- No clear optimal point exists (flat or monotonic relationship)

## Expected Outcomes

Based on H1.470.1 results:
- **640**: Slightly worse than 768 (too constrained)
- **704**: Close to 768 performance
- **768**: Peak multi-step performance
- **832**: Slight degradation from peak
- **896**: Further degradation, trending toward 1024 pattern

## Implications

If supported:
- Confirms existence of optimal representation dimension
- Suggests CG architecture has "sweet spot" for multi-step reasoning
- Provides guidance for architecture design: aim for ~768 dimensions

If refuted:
- Optimal dimension may be different than 768
- Relationship may be more complex than simple peak
- May need to investigate other factors (ratio, architecture, etc.)
