# H1.470.1.1.1: Even finer dimension sweep around 832

## Hypothesis

Optimal representation dimension is ~832 for CG on multi-step tasks.

## Background

From H1.470.1.1 (fine-grained sweep around 768):
- 832 shows best multi-step performance (+41.49% improvement, -1.76% gap)
- 768 only achieves +31.06% multi-step (3rd best out of 5)
- High variance at 768 (8.79-9.36%) suggests instability
- At 832, CG performs BETTER on multi-step than single-step (negative gap)

## Prediction

Even finer sweep around 832 [800, 816, 832, 848, 864] will show:
1. Peak multi-step improvement around 832
2. Single-step improvement may continue increasing with dimension (overfitting)
3. Improvement gap (single-to-multi) minimized around 832
4. Performance drops significantly away from 832

## Test Design

Compare CG with dimensions [800, 816, 832, 848, 864] on:
- Single-step tasks (1-step prediction)
- Multi-step tasks (3-step prediction)

15 epochs, 800 train / 200 test samples per dimension, 3 runs per dimension.

## Expected Outcomes

If hypothesis is SUPPORTED:
- 832 will show highest multi-step improvement (>41.49%)
- Improvement gap will be minimal or negative at 832
- Performance will drop monotonically away from 832

If hypothesis is REFUTED:
- Another dimension (800, 816, 848, or 864) will show better multi-step performance
- 832 may not be the true optimum
- The pattern may be more complex than a single peak

## Success Criteria

Multi-step improvement at 832 > all other dimensions AND > previous best (41.49%).
